Professional trip highlight video maker and journey route animator app

How Our Travel Recap App Works

The trip animation app uses intelligent algorithms to scan your photos' GPS data and automatically detect your trips. The journey route animator then creates smooth, cinematic travel route animations showing your path across cities, countries, and continents. Whether you're creating a quick weekend trip highlight video or a year-long travel recap video, the animated travel map app makes it effortless.

Extensive Customization for Your Trip Animation Videos

The travel story maker app offers professional-level customization options to make your animated travel map videos truly unique:

  • •Choose between 30 FPS, 60 FPS, or premium 90 FPS for silky-smooth travel route animations in your trip highlight videos
  • •Adjust video quality (bitrate) from normal to high for crystal-clear map journey videos without visual artifacts
  • •Select from multiple stunning earth styles including Realistic, Night Lights, Day Map, and Satellite views for your animated travel map videos
  • •Toggle country borders on your journey route animator with customizable colors and thickness for enhanced geography visualization
  • •Create trip animation videos in Vertical (9:16), Widescreen (16:9), Cinema (2.35:1), or Square (1:1) formats perfect for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and other social platforms
  • •Control the journey route animator speed between locations (up to 3500 km/s) to create dynamic or relaxed travel recap videos
  • •Adjust pause duration at each location (0.5-5 seconds) to highlight important destinations in your trip highlight videos
  • •Display City, Region, and/or Country labels on your animated travel map videos to provide context for your journey
  • •Toggle whether to show all past trip paths or just the current trip in your travel route animation
